\chapter{Mathematical Formulation of BEM}\label{chapter:mathematical-formulation-bem}

Before we discuss the implementation of the \ac{BEM} for the Acoustic Helmholtz equation, we first need to discuss the Acoustic Wave equation. We can then show how the mathematical formulation of the \ac{BIE} arises from the Acoustic Helmholtz equation. Finally, we will discuss the Burton-Miller formulation to address the issue of fictitious frequencies.
